GREENFIELD DENTAL HOSPITAL
Appointment Policy

Patients with scheduled appointments should arrive 15 minutes before their appointment time.

Patients who expect to be late should contact the hospital as soon as possible.

A patient arriving more than 30 minutes after the scheduled appointment time may need to be rescheduled depending on clinic capacity.

Patients who cannot attend an appointment should cancel as early as possible so the appointment slot can be offered to another patient.

Emergency dental cases may be assessed separately from routine scheduled appointments.

Appointment times are estimates and may change when emergency cases require immediate attention.
