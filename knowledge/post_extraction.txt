Post-Extraction Patient Instructions

After a tooth extraction, the patient should bite firmly on the provided gauze for 30 to 45 minutes.

The patient should not rinse the mouth vigorously, spit forcefully, or use a straw during the first 24 hours after extraction.

Hot food and drinks should be avoided for the first 4 hours. The patient may eat soft, cool foods when comfortable.

The patient should avoid smoking and alcohol for at least 48 hours after the procedure.

After 24 hours, the patient may gently rinse the mouth with warm salt water. Vigorous rinsing should still be avoided.

Mild discomfort and slight bleeding may occur after an extraction. If bleeding remains heavy despite applying firm pressure with clean gauze, the patient should contact the dental clinic.

The patient should return to the hospital if there is persistent heavy bleeding, increasing facial swelling, difficulty breathing, or any other concerning symptoms.

Medication should only be taken according to the instructions provided by the treating dental professional.
