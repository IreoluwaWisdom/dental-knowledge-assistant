GREENFIELD DENTAL HOSPITAL
Emergency Dental Care Policy

Patients reporting difficulty breathing associated with facial or oral swelling should receive immediate clinical attention.

Uncontrolled or persistent heavy bleeding should be treated as an urgent dental problem.

Dental trauma involving significant injury to the mouth or teeth should be assessed promptly.

Severe dental pain without other emergency features should still be assessed, but the final urgency level must be determined by an appropriate dental professional.

The system must not independently diagnose the patient's condition or replace clinical assessment.s
